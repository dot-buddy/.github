# Code of Conduct

Dot Buddy is a small, friendly project. We want everyone who files an issue, draws a character or sends a pull request to feel welcome, whatever their experience, background or language.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to open source.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Assume good intent.
- Respect that people draw in different styles. A character can be simple and still be welcome.
- Credit other people's work, and respect a creator's wish to have their work taken down.

## What isn't okay

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexual content or imagery, and anything hateful or discriminatory, in issues, pull requests or characters.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly pushing on someone after they've asked you to stop.

## Reporting

If something here makes you uncomfortable, report it privately: on the repository's **Security** tab, choose **Report a vulnerability** and say it's a conduct report. Only the maintainers can see it. For content that breaks GitHub's own rules you can also use GitHub's **Report content**.

The maintainers will look at every report, keep the reporter's identity private, and may edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests or characters, or block someone from the project.

## Where this applies

In the Dot Buddy repositories and anywhere someone is representing the project.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
